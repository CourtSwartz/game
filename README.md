# ⚡ Power in the Streets

**Power in the Streets** is a browser-based strategy game where you lead a grassroots movement to fight for housing, education, or healthcare justice. Choose your cause, pick a hero, and build political power, legal progress, and community support — before you burn out or run out of funds!

🌐 [Play Now on GitHub Pages](https://yourusername.github.io/power-in-the-streets/)  
🎮 Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript  
🖼️ Featuring original character artwork and mechanics

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

- Select a cause: 🏘 Housing, 📚 Education, or 🏥 Healthcare
- Choose a hero:
  - **Amira** the Organizer (+5 🫂 Community Support)
  - **Dante** the Lawyer (+5 ⚖️ Legal Progress)
  - **Maya** the Activist (+5 💬 Political Power)
  - **Leo** the Student (+5 🔋 Energy)
- Make choices each turn:
  - Take political action, pursue legal cases, or organize community work
  - Fundraise, rest, or eat food to regain resources
- Win by hitting **25 in all three stats**
- Lose by burning out, going broke, or failing too often
